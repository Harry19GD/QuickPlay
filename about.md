# Quick Play

Skip the menu maze. Pin the levels you're currently progressing to a Quick
Play list, then launch them straight from the main menu with two taps.

- **Quick Play button**: instantly play levels on-the-go.
- **Quick Play popup**: your recently played levels. Choose one
  to jump straight in.
- **Pin from any level**: open a level's info screen and tap the **+**
  button to add it. Remove entries anytime from the popup.

Your list is saved between sessions, which means it persists on each game exit. 
Levels that can no longer be found (deleted, un-saved) are pruned automatically 
with a friendly heads-up.

**Make sure you have Node IDs mod installed!**
